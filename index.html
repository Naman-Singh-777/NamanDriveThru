Use the on-screen arrows to accelerate, reverse and steer. Tap BRAKE to stop.

Step 2 of 4

Camera

Default view: the camera follows your car from behind.

Press O to rise into an overview of the whole map. Press O again to drop back behind the car.

The overview camera needs the O key, so it isn't available on a phone or tablet.

Each checkpoint opens a menu. Long menus scroll: use the mouse wheel or trackpad.swipe up and down. Close a menu with the X or by clickingtapping outside it, or press Esc once.

Fullscreen: the game goes fullscreen when your browser allows it. Esc closes menus without leaving fullscreen. To exit fullscreen, press Esc 3 times.

Fullscreen: it starts on your first tap where your browser allows it.

Stuck, glitched or fell off the map? Reverse, hold the brake, or refresh the page. Use the browser's refresh button or Ctrl+Shift+R.Use your browser's normal reload. It happens. The physics and I are still working things out.
